policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Short version: cancel any time before production starts on February 1, 2027 for a full refund. After that, your piece is being made for you and the order is final, except for defects, damage or lost parcels.

How the presale works

Drop 01 is a pre-order. You're charged at checkout, we produce your hoodie after the presale closes, and it ships by March 15, 2027. Each piece is produced for an actual order, which is why our refund terms are tied to the production date.

Cancel before production: full refund

You can cancel your order for a full refund, including shipping, at any time before production starts on February 1, 2027. Email hello@takemyhoodie.com with your PayPal order ID. Refunds go back to your original payment method through PayPal, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Size or color changes

You can change your size or color for free before February 1, 2027. Email us your order ID and the new choice.

After production starts

From February 1, 2027, orders can't be cancelled, and we don't accept returns or exchanges for fit or change of mind. Please check the size guide before ordering. Have a sizing question? Ask us first, we reply within 48 hours.

If we're late

If we can't ship your order by March 15, 2027, we'll email you a new date. If the new date doesn't work for you, you can cancel for a full refund.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

If your hoodie arrives damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email hello@takemyhoodie.com within 14 days of delivery with your order ID and photos. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, and cover any return shipping.

Lost parcels

If your parcel is confirmed lost by the carrier, we'll reship or refund in full.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a PayPal dispute or chargeback. We'll sort it out faster directly.
