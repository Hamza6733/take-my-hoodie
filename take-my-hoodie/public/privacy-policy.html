Privacy
policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what personal information TAKE MY HOODIE ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website or place an order, and how we use it. Questions? Email hello@takemyhoodie.com.

What we collect

  • Order information: when you buy, PayPal shares with us your name, email address, shipping address and the details of your order (size, color, quantity, amount paid).
  • Payment information: payments are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card number or bank details.
  • Waitlist: if you sign up for Drop 02 updates, we store your email address.
  • Contact messages: if you use our contact form or email us, we keep the messages and the details you include.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider (Netlify) may log basic technical information such as IP address and browser type for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

  • To process, produce and ship your order and send order updates and tracking.
  • To answer your questions and handle cancellations, refunds and support.
  • To email you about future drops, only if you joined the waitlist. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or just reply "unsubscribe".
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • PayPal, to process payments (PayPal privacy statement).
  • Netlify, which hosts this site and stores waitlist and contact form submissions.
  • Shipping carriers, who need your name and address to deliver your order.
  • Our production partner, only the order details needed to make your hoodie (never your payment details).
  • Authorities, when required by law.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. PayPal sets its own cookies when the checkout loads, to process payments securely and prevent fraud. Google Fonts is used to display our typefaces.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes. Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email hello@takemyhoodie.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

International transfers

We sell worldwide, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, by us and the providers listed above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
