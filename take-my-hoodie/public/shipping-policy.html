Shipping
policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Presale: every Drop 01 order ships by March 15, 2027. You pay today, we produce your hoodie, then it ships with tracking.

When your order ships

Drop 01 is made to order. Production starts after the presale closes, and all orders ship by March 15, 2027. If anything changes our timeline, we'll email every customer right away with a new date and the option to cancel for a full refund.

Shipping cost

Free worldwide shipping. The shipping cost (if any) is shown at checkout before you pay. One shipping charge per order, no matter how many hoodies.

Where we ship

Worldwide. If PayPal accepts your shipping address at checkout, we ship there. If we discover we can't deliver to your address, we'll refund you in full.

Delivery times

Estimated delivery after dispatch:

These are estimates from our carriers. Delays from customs, weather or holidays are outside our control.

Tracking

Every order includes tracking. You'll receive your tracking number by email the day your hoodie ships.

Shipping address

We ship to the address you enter in PayPal at checkout. Double-check it. Need to change it? Email hello@takemyhoodie.com with your order ID before your order ships.

Customs, duties & taxes

Orders shipped outside the United States may be charged import duties, taxes or customs fees by the destination country. These are not included in our prices or shipping cost and are the buyer's responsibility. Refused parcels due to unpaid duties are not eligible for a refund of shipping costs.

Lost or damaged parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 15 business days, or your parcel arrives damaged, email hello@takemyhoodie.com with your order ID and photos (for damage). We'll work with the carrier and send a replacement or refund.
