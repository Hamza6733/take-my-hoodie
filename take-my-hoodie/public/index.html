How does it fit?

Oversized and boxy by design. Take your usual size for the intended oversized look, or size down for a closer fit. Measure a hoodie you already own and compare it with our .

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes, you can cancel for a full refund any time before production starts on February 1, 2027. Just email us your order number.

Once production starts, each piece is made for an order, so we can't cancel it. If your hoodie arrives damaged or defective, we'll replace it or refund you. Full details in our refund policy.

Do I need a PayPal account?

No. Hit "Debit or Credit Card" at checkout to pay as a guest. PayPal just processes the payment securely. We never see your card details.

Can I change my size after ordering?

Yes, as long as it's before production starts on February 1, 2027. Email us your order number and the new size.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@takemyhoodie.com or use our contact page. We reply within 48 hours. You can also DM us on Instagram or TikTok.
